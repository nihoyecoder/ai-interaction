What the extension should report

Open the side panel, choose Select Design, then a mode.

Section

  1. The big headline: section.hero, the whole gradient band. ↑ gives main. ↓ from the hero gives div.container, the centered column.
  2. A link in the header: header.site-header.
  3. A grey logo box: section.logos.
  4. A feature card: section.features.
  5. A pricing plan: div.block-pricing, a section made of plain divs.
  6. The white strip just above the grey pricing band: the nearest section, section.features or div.block-pricing.
  7. A question, or the embedded frame: section.faq.
  8. The dark "Ready to start?" band: section.cta.
  9. A footer link: footer.site-footer.
  10. The dark bar at the very top: div.announcement.

Element

  1. The headline: h1. ↑ gives div.hero-copy.
  2. The text of the "New in 2.0" badge: demo-badge, a web component. Its small blue dot is inside the component's shadow root: span.dot, and ↑ gives span.pill.
  3. A feature card's description: p. ↑ gives article.feature-card: the link around it has the same box, so they're one step.
  4. The embedded frame: iframe.map. Nothing inside it can be picked.

Full page

Ten sections, in this order: div.announcement, header.site-header (sticky), section.hero, section.logos, section.features, div.block-pricing, section.faq, section.cta, footer.site-footer, and this checklist, aside.test-notes.
